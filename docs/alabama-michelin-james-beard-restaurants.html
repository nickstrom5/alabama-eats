Alabama MICHELIN and James Beard restaurants

23 Alabama restaurants with a MICHELIN Guide distinction (5 Bib Gourmands and 12 Recommendeds) or a James Beard honor (1 America's Classic, 2 finalists, 7 semifinalists and 2 winners). No Alabama restaurant has a MICHELIN star.

What counts as an honor here

Two sources, reported as facts rather than scores. The MICHELIN Guide American South (its second selection, October 2026) lists 17 Alabama restaurants: Bib Gourmands such as La Fête, Bayonet and Pizza Grace in Birmingham and the Noble South in Mobile, and Recommended places from Bottega to 1856 in Auburn. The James Beard Foundation named the Bright Star in Bessemer an America's Classic in 2010, gave Chris Hastings (Hot and Hot Fish Club, 2012) and Adam Evans (Automatic Seafood and Oysters, 2022) Best Chef: South, and made Bottega a 2026 finalist for Outstanding Hospitality.
